Refund Policy

Effective 10 October 2026. Heat Check sells two digital products: the Date Night Pass (4 hours of paid access) and Pro Lifetime (a one-time purchase). Current prices are shown in the app before you pay. Both are flirty party games for couples, 18+, delivered instantly to your account.

When you get a full refund

  • You were charged but didn't get access. If your payment went through and your account didn't unlock within 24 hours, we'll unlock it or refund you in full.
  • You were charged twice for the same purchase. We refund the duplicate in full.
  • Pro Lifetime, within 7 days. Changed your mind? Ask within 7 days of purchase and we'll refund it in full. Paid access on the account ends when the refund is issued.

When refunds aren't available

  • Date Night Pass after it starts. A pass starts the moment payment is confirmed and lasts 4 hours, so it can't be refunded once it has begun, except for the technical problems above.
  • Lifetime after 7 days, except for the technical problems above.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5 to 7 business days to show them.

Restoring a purchase

A new phone or cleared browser doesn't need a refund: sign in with the same email and your access comes back.
