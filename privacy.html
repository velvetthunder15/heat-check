Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026. Heat Check is a pass-the-phone party game for adults. It is built so that the things you say, do and type while playing never leave your phone.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email collection.
  • Player names, scores and settings live only in your browser.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data. No ads. No tracking cookies.
  • The only things that reach a server are the normal requests needed to load the site, plus anonymous crash reports.

1. Information stored on your device

To remember your game between sessions, Heat Check saves the following in your browser's local storage on your own device:

  • Player names and couple pairings you enter
  • Scores, heat level, penalty mode and other settings
  • That you confirmed you are 18 or older and accepted the consent note

This data is never uploaded to us. It stays until you tap "New night", clear your browser data, or uninstall the app. Anyone with your unlocked phone can see it, so use a nickname if you prefer.

2. Things you type during play

Answers typed in games like Hot Seat are held in memory only while that round is on screen. They are not saved and never sent anywhere.

3. Information sent to servers

Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any website, Vercel's servers receive your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver them, and may keep short-lived logs for security and reliability.

Fonts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address. Google's privacy policy applies to those requests.

Crash reports. If the app hits an error, it may send a short technical report: the error message, the file and line it happened on, the page path and a timestamp. Reports never include player names, scores, card answers or anything you typed.

4. What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera. The site's security settings block access to the camera, microphone and location outright.

5. Cookies

Heat Check does not set cookies. It uses local storage (described above) and a service worker cache so the game works offline.

6. How long we keep data

On-device data stays until you remove it. Crash reports and hosting logs are kept only as long as needed to fix problems and keep the service secure, and no longer than 90 days.

7. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with strict security headers that block framing, untrusted scripts and third-party data collection. Because there are no accounts and no server-side database of players, there is no personal data on our side to breach.

8. Adults only

Heat Check is intended only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Your rights

You are in full control of the data stored on your device and can delete it at any time from the home screen ("New night") or your browser settings. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you can also ask about or object to any processing described here.

10. Changes

If we add features such as a paid tier that need new data, this policy will be updated before those features go live, and the effective date above will change.
